core14.bin is generated from Adobe's Core 14 AFM files (Courier, Helvetica, Times, Symbol and
ZapfDingbats, version 4.1, 1997), as mirrored by Apache PDFBox
(pdfbox/src/main/resources/org/apache/pdfbox/resources/afm).

Modifications: only the font-wide metrics and the character metrics (code, width, glyph name)
are kept, converted to a compact binary format and compressed with Deflate. The AFM files
of Symbol and ZapfDingbats have no Ascender, Descender, CapHeight or XHeight: these values
are derived from the font and glyph bounding boxes. The copyright notices of the AFM files
are retained below.
